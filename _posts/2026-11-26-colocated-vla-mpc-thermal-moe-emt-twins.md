## At a glance

- **Sharing one Jetson GPU can cut [VLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vision-language-action_model "Vision-language-action (VLA) model: a policy that maps camera input and language to robot actions.")–[MPC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_predictive_control "Model predictive control (MPC): a high-frequency controller that repeatedly solves a short-horizon optimization to generate actuator commands.") energy and raise control QoS.** [Dubot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30177 "Guo et al., Resource-Aware VLA–MPC Co-Location for Efficient Embodied AI, arXiv 2611.30177, submitted 23 November 2026. A preprint, not peer reviewed.") co-locates VLA inference with 500 Hz MPC on a [Jetson AGX Orin](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/autonomous-machines/embedded-systems/jetson-orin/ "NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin: an embedded computer for robots and edge AI; here run in a 60 W power mode.") and a [Jetson AGX Thor](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/autonomous-machines/embedded-systems/jetson-thor/ "NVIDIA Jetson AGX Thor: a high-end embedded computer for robots and edge AI; here run in a 120 W power mode."), reporting up to 1.41× / 1.35× cycle speedup, 16.1% / 12.4% lower GPU energy, and deadline hit rates from 61.7% / 63.7% to over 99%. Energy is the GPU rail; the loop is [MuJoCo](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco "MuJoCo: a physics simulator used here as the closed-loop plant, not a physical robot."), not a physical robot.
- **A thermal [MoE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixture_of_experts "Mixture-of-experts (MoE): a model that routes each token to a subset of expert networks rather than using the full model on every token.") runtime lowers GPU temperature and watts; the aging claim is modeled.** [GreenDynaMix](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31737 "Lin et al., GreenDynaMix: Thermal-Aware Dynamic Mixed-Precision MoE LLM Inference Systems, arXiv 2611.31737, submitted 24 November 2026. A preprint, not peer reviewed.") pairs [DVFS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamic_voltage_scaling "Dynamic voltage and frequency scaling (DVFS): runtime adjustment of a processor’s clock and voltage, used here as a thermal controller.") with mixed-precision experts on an [L40S](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/l40s/ "NVIDIA L40S: a 48 GB datacenter GPU used as the paper’s server platform."), an RTX 4090 and a Jetson Orin NX, cutting measured average GPU power 27% and 17% on the first two boards. The 25–33% “aging-rate” cut is an Arrhenius factor, not a measured lifetime.
- **A data-center [EMT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMTP "Electromagnetic transient (EMT): a fast electrical transient in a power network; EMTP is a standard class of EMT simulation tools.") “twin” still tracks a simulator.** [Jena et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32882 "Jena, Wang, Chen, Chadwell, Yancey and Xie, Adaptive Electromagnetic Transient-level Digital Twins for Data Centers via In-Context Learning with LLMs, arXiv 2611.32882, submitted 24 November 2026. A preprint, under review.") adapt a GPT-2 in-context learner to unseen [PSCAD](https://www.pscad.com/ "PSCAD: a commercial electromagnetic-transient simulator used here as the ground-truth plant model.") configurations from 36 samples, with [PCC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point_of_common_coupling "Point of common coupling (PCC): the electrical boundary where a facility connects to the utility grid.")-voltage MAE of 0.00078 / 0.00152 / 0.00119 at 12.92× PSCAD. There is no plant telemetry, no public code, and no closed control loop.

## Key developments

### 1. Co-locating VLA inference with 500 Hz MPC cuts GPU energy and deadline misses · submitted 23 Nov 2026 · academia / robot compute

Guo, Lou, Zhao and colleagues at [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/ "Shanghai Jiao Tong University."), the [Shanghai Qi Zhi Institute](https://www.sqz.ac.cn/en "Shanghai Qi Zhi Institute (上海期智研究院), an AI research institute in Shanghai.") and the [Institute of Computing Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Computing_Technology,_Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences "Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences.") present Dubot (arXiv [2611.30177](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30177 "Guo et al., arXiv 2611.30177, a preprint, not peer reviewed.")). It co-locates VLA inference and 500 Hz MPC on one mobile GPU, selecting among SM partitioning, stream overlap and intra-block fusion. Orin runs at 60 W / 1.30 GHz and Thor at 120 W / 1.53 GHz; OpenVLA, OpenVLA-OFT and UVA are BF16, MPC is FP32. Versus a sequential GPU baseline, the [HTML full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.30177 "Source of the 1.41× / 1.35× speedups, 16.1% / 12.4% GPU-energy cuts, 61.7% / 63.7% baseline QoS, and the GPU-sensor energy method.") reports up to 1.41× / 1.35× cycle speedup, 16.1% / 12.4% lower GPU energy from the built-in GPU power sensor, and deadline hit rates rising from 61.7% / 63.7% to over 99% on both boards and all three models. The loop is MuJoCo, not a physical robot. Energy is GPU-rail only, with no board-level joules or thermal traces. The header lists [ISCA](https://iscaconf.org/ "IEEE/ACM International Symposium on Computer Architecture.") 2027 with a placeholder DOI; treat that venue as author-claimed. No public repository was found.

Implication: For on-board robot computers, a schedule that meets the control deadline can also spend fewer GPU joules per cycle, but board-level watts and heat remain unmeasured.

**Sources:** [arXiv 2611.30177](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30177 "Abstract and bibliographic record.") and the [HTML full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.30177 "Measurement setup, energy method, and the Orin/Thor figures cited above.").

### 2. DVFS plus mixed-precision experts cools MoE inference; the aging claim is a model · submitted 24 Nov 2026 · academia / adjacent thermal

Lin, Griperay, Zhou, Bonacci and Hu at the [University of Pittsburgh](https://www.pitt.edu/ "University of Pittsburgh.") present GreenDynaMix (arXiv [2611.31737](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31737 "Lin et al., arXiv 2611.31737, a preprint, not peer reviewed.")). A thermal PID scales GPU frequency; a second loop recovers throughput by changing how many experts run in high precision, guided by router weights. It is evaluated, with no platform-specific retuning, in [llama-cli](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp "llama.cpp / llama-cli: an open LLM inference runtime used for the paper’s batch-1 sessions.") at batch 1, primarily on [OLMoE-1B-7B](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.02060 "OLMoE-1B-7B: an open mixture-of-experts language model with 1 billion active and 7 billion total parameters; the paper’s primary workload.") (8-bit/4-bit on the L40S and 4090; 4-bit/2-bit on the 16 GB Orin NX). The [HTML full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.31737 "Source of Table I temperatures, throughput and perplexity, the 228.7 W to 167.9 W and 265.5 W to 220.8 W power measurements, and the 42.1% memory overhead.") reports average GPU temperature falling from 45.8 °C to 41.6 °C, 60.3 °C to 54.8 °C and 70.0 °C to 65.1 °C, with throughput nearly unchanged. Measured average GPU power falls from 228.7 W to 167.9 W (27%) on the L40S and from 265.5 W to 220.8 W (17%) on the 4090; Orin NX board power is not reported. Perplexity rises 1.6% and 0.9% on the first two boards, and 9.4% on Orin NX, where preloading both precisions also grows the OLMoE footprint from 7,973 MB to 11,333 MB (+42.1%). The 25–33% aging-rate reduction is a modeled Arrhenius factor, not a measured lifetime. No public code was found. This is a GPU runtime, not a hall twin.

Implication: Temperature and tokens/s can be controlled together if precision is the slack variable, but the reliability benefit is a thermal-acceleration model, and the memory cost is material on small boards.

**Sources:** [arXiv 2611.31737](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31737 "Abstract: 25–33% aging-rate reduction and the 0.9–1.6% / 9.4% perplexity costs.") and the [HTML full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.31737 "Table I and the measured power and memory figures.").

### 3. An adaptive EMT digital twin of a data-center PCC is still a PSCAD surrogate · submitted 24 Nov 2026 · academia / digital twin

Jena at [Texas A&M](https://www.tamu.edu/ "Texas A&M University."), Wang at [ISO New England](https://www.iso-ne.com/ "ISO New England: the independent system operator for the six New England states."), colleagues at [Electric Power Engineers](https://epeconsulting.com/ "Electric Power Engineers (EPE): a power-systems consulting firm in Austin, Texas.") and Xie at [Harvard](https://seas.harvard.edu/ "Harvard John A. Paulson School of Engineering and Applied Sciences.") (arXiv [2611.32882](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32882 "Jena et al., arXiv 2611.32882, a preprint, under review.")) train a 6-layer GPT-2 in-context learner on 1,000 PSCAD configurations of an IEEE 39-bus system plus a 100 MVA data-center interconnect, then adapt at test time from 36 post-disturbance samples. The IT load is a lumped PQ source, nominally 25 MW and varied over [0, 320] MW; UPS and point-of-load dynamics are omitted, and the islanded battery is treated as infinite. Table IV in the [HTML full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.32882 "Source of Table IV MAE/RMSE, 9.911 s versus 128.06 s, and the non-adaptive baseline comparison.") reports PCC-voltage MAE of 0.00078, 0.00152 and 0.00119 on step, ramp and mode-switch cases, at 9.911 s versus 128.06 s for PSCAD (12.92×). A proposed point-on-wave telemetry path is not demonstrated. There is no public code, no telemetry calibration, and no closed control loop. This is a simulation-only electrical twin, not a hall model.

Implication: Inference-time adaptation is a credible direction for a facility-level power twin, but a surrogate that tracks PSCAD is not yet a predictor or controller of a live hall.

**Sources:** [arXiv 2611.32882](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32882 "Abstract and bibliographic record.") and the [HTML full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.32882 "Plant assumptions, Table IV, and Remark 2 on a possible telemetry path.").

## Bottom line

On-board robot computers can spend fewer GPU joules per VLA–MPC cycle when the two workloads share the GPU, if the control deadline is treated as a hard constraint; board-level heat is still unmeasured here. Thermal DVFS plus mixed-precision experts can cut measured GPU watts and temperature with little throughput loss, at a memory cost and with only a modeled reliability gain. The only new data-center twin in this window is an EMT surrogate of PSCAD, not a telemetry-closed plant model.
